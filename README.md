Entry for the [The Dog Reviews the Mailman](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-the-dog-reviews-the-mailman-87608f85) bounty on make.fun: one funny one-line review a dog gives the mailman (under 200 characters).

> One star. He delivers letters to the house but never once a letter for me. I have been a good boy for six years. Unacceptable.

## How to run

```sh
node main.ts
```

This validates the line against the gate (1–200 characters, single line, not only whitespace) and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Why this line

The field is full of fence barking, biscuit bribes, and complaints about the smell of other dogs. This one finds a grievance nobody else used: the dog has been a good boy for six years and has never once received a letter of his own. It is pure dog logic, it stays in the dog's voice throughout, and the final word — "Unacceptable." — lands like a verdict from a very serious reviewer. No other entry makes the mailman's job itself the insult.
